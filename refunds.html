Refunds and cancellations.

Effective October 31, 2026

What you pay for, how to cancel, and when money or credits come back. This page restates the Terms of Use; if the two ever differ, the Terms govern.

What you pay for

Browsing and a free account cost nothing. Membership is $22 a month and includes 8 seat credits and 3 gift credits each month. Extra seat credits are $2 each.

  • Your card is charged only for what you choose to buy: the membership, extra seat credits and tickets. A seat, a no-show or a late cancel never puts a charge on your card.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Cancelling membership

  • Membership renews each month until you cancel.
  • Cancelling runs to the end of the month you paid for, and that month's seats stay usable until then.
  • There is no refund on membership.
  • The credits included with membership are for that month and do not roll over. Extra seat credits you buy do not expire and are not refunded.

Seats and credits

A seat at an event costs a credit, not money. There is no card charge, no deposit and nothing to refund.

  • Release your seat before the event's cutoff and the credit comes back. After the cutoff, the credit is spent.
  • If the table does not go ahead, or the venue cancels, the credit comes back.

Tickets

A ticket is a purchase from the host who runs the event, not from OpenSeat. Your payment goes to the host's own Stripe account.

  • A ticket is charged in full when you buy it.
  • If an event does not go ahead, including one that misses its minimum number of guests, every buyer is refunded in full automatically. If the host cancels, you are refunded automatically. A host can also refund buyers at any time.
  • "Refunded" means every cent you paid comes back.
  • Can't make it? You can hand your ticket to someone you name, free.
  • Beyond that, the refund policy is the host's.

Disputes

OpenSeat does not decide who is at fault in a disagreement between a host and a buyer. A dispute over a charge goes through Stripe and your card issuer.

Contact

Questions about a charge, a refund or cancelling: accounts@openseat.ca.
