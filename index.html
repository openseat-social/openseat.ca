The software behind the seat.

The web app runs at app.openseat.ca, in beta. The stack today:

Application
One TypeScript codebase: React web app, Fastify API on Node.js, a background worker. PostgreSQL 16.
Hosting
Docker on our own Linode server.
Payments
Stripe for memberships and tickets; Stripe Connect pays hosts. A refund reads complete only once the provider confirms the money moved.
Privacy
Members choose what to share, item by item, at setup, and can download everything OpenSeat holds about them. Names stay private until people meet at a table.
Sign-in
Email links, Google, Facebook, passkeys.
Assistant
Eleanor runs on Claude through the Anthropic API, with a daily spending limit per member. A move to Amazon Bedrock is planned and budgeted: code merged, migration ahead.

Before you take a seat.

What is OpenSeat?

An app for adults to meet new people over food and drinks. Claim a seat at an event at a real place, or start a table yourself.

What does it cost?

Browsing and an account are free. Membership is $22 a month with 8 seat credits and 3 gift credits. Extra seat credits are $2. Hosts sell tickets to their own events.

Can I release a seat I claimed?

Yes. Before the event's cutoff the credit comes back; after it, the credit is spent. If a table does not go ahead, it comes back.

What if an event is full?

A full event keeps a line. People who ask to hear about a freed seat are told in order.

Can I bring someone?

Yes. A gift credit brings someone along or gives someone a seat. Membership includes 3 a month.

Who will be at the table?

People who claimed or were given a seat. An event can carry a recommended age range as a guide. Names show once you have met.

How is my data handled?

You choose what to share, item by item, at setup. Strangers cannot see your name until you have met. You can see and delete what Eleanor has learned about you, and download everything OpenSeat holds about you.

What is a Tribe?

A host's audience, like an email list. Free to join. Hosts can charge dues through their own Stripe account; OpenSeat's fee on dues is 0%.

Where is OpenSeat available?

OpenSeat is in beta and has not launched. Launch plans have not been announced. Questions: accounts@openseat.ca.

Who runs OpenSeat?

OpenSeat LLC, a self-funded company. Stephen Iervella is founder and CEO. Reach the company at accounts@openseat.ca.
