Eleanor helps with the plans.

Every member's assistant starts as Eleanor. You can change her name and picture.

She helps you find events and start tables. She works with what you tell her and what each event's public page shows.

She does

  • Speak as herself
  • Find events and start tables
  • Show you each thing she has learned about you, and delete it when you ask

She does not

  • Write your messages, coach you or suggest replies
  • Join your conversations with other people
  • Know what happened at the table, or who said what

The software behind the seat.

The web app runs at app.openseat.ca and is in active development. This is the stack today, including the planned move for the assistant.

Application
One TypeScript codebase: a React web app, a Fastify API on Node.js and a background worker. PostgreSQL 16 stores the data.
Hosting
Docker on our own Linode server.
Payments
Stripe handles memberships and tickets. Stripe Connect pays hosts. A refund is marked complete only after the payment provider confirms the money moved.
Privacy
Members choose what to share, item by item, during setup. Anyone can download everything OpenSeat holds about them. Strangers' names stay private until they have met at a table.
Sign-in
Email sign-in links, Google, Facebook and passkeys.
Assistant
Eleanor runs on Claude through the Anthropic API, with a daily spending limit per member. The move to Amazon Bedrock is planned and budgeted; the code is merged and the migration is ahead.

Before you take a seat.

What is OpenSeat?

An app for meeting new people over food and drinks, for adults. You browse events at real places, claim a seat and meet the people at the table. You can also start a table yourself.

What does it cost?

Browsing and an account are free. Membership is $22 a month and includes 8 seat credits and 3 gift credits each month. Extra seat credits are $2 each. Hosts also sell tickets to their own events.

Can I release a seat I claimed?

Yes, until the cutoff shown on the event. Release it before then and the credit comes back. After the cutoff the credit is spent. If a table does not go ahead, the credit comes back.

What if an event is full?

A full event keeps a line. People who ask to be told about a freed seat are told in order.

Can I bring someone?

Yes. Gift credits let you bring someone along or give someone a seat. Membership includes 3 gift credits each month.

Who will be at the table?

People who claimed a seat, or were given one. Each event can carry a recommended age range as a guide. Names show once you have met at a table together.

How is my data handled?

You choose what to share, item by item, during setup. Strangers cannot see your name until you have met at a table. You can see and delete what Eleanor has learned about you, and download everything OpenSeat holds about you.

What is a Tribe?

A host's audience on OpenSeat, like an email list. Joining is free. Hosts can charge dues through their own Stripe account, and OpenSeat's fee on those dues is 0%.

Where is OpenSeat available?

The app is in active development and has not launched. Launch plans have not been announced. Write to accounts@openseat.ca with any questions.

Who runs OpenSeat?

OpenSeat LLC, a self-funded company. Stephen Iervella is its founder and CEO. You can reach the company at accounts@openseat.ca.
