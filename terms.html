Who we are

OpenSeat is run by OpenSeat LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. "We" and "us" on this page means OpenSeat LLC. "You" means anyone who uses OpenSeat at openseat.ca or app.openseat.ca.

By creating an account or using OpenSeat, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

Who can join

OpenSeat is for adults. You must be 18 or older to have an account. We ask your date of birth the first time you claim a seat, and we rely on your answer. It is entered once; a correction goes through support.

Your account

  • One account per person. Sign in with an emailed link, Google, Facebook or a passkey.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. What happens on your account is on you.
  • We stop duplicate accounts using your card and your device. Those markers stay after an account is closed, so a closed account cannot be used to start over around a block or a no-show hold.

Membership and credits

Browsing and a free account cost nothing. Membership is $22 a month and includes 8 seat credits and 3 gift credits each month. Extra seat credits are $2 each.

  • Your card is charged only for what you choose to buy: the membership, extra seat credits and tickets. A seat, a no-show or a late cancel never puts a charge on your card.
  • Membership renews each month until you cancel. Cancelling runs to the end of the month you paid for, and that month's seats stay usable until then. There is no refund on membership.
  • The credits included with membership are for that month and do not roll over. Extra seat credits you buy do not expire and are not refunded.
  • Membership is sold on the website, not through an app store.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Seats

A seat at an event is part of membership. It costs a credit, not money. There is no card charge, no deposit and nothing to refund.

  • Claiming a seat holds one credit. One seat per person per event, and up to 10 seat claims in any 7 days.
  • Release your seat before the event's cutoff and the credit comes back. After the cutoff, the credit is spent.
  • If the table does not go ahead, or the venue cancels, the credit comes back.
  • A gift credit brings someone along or gives someone a seat.
  • At the table, everyone pays their own bill. OpenSeat does not touch it.

Tickets

Some events are run by a host who sells tickets. A ticket is a purchase from that host, not from OpenSeat.

  • The host is the seller. Your payment goes to the host's own Stripe account, and the host is paid through Stripe Connect. OpenSeat takes no fee on tickets today.
  • A ticket is charged in full when you buy it.
  • If an event does not go ahead, including one that misses its minimum number of guests, every buyer is refunded in full automatically. If the host cancels, you are refunded automatically. A host can also refund buyers at any time.
  • "Refunded" means every cent you paid comes back.
  • Can't make it? You can hand your ticket to someone you name, free.
  • Beyond that, the refund policy is the host's. OpenSeat does not decide who is at fault in a disagreement between a host and a buyer. A dispute over a charge goes through Stripe and your card issuer.

Proposing, hosting and Tribes

  • Starting a table makes you its proposer, with no special powers over it. You can change or cancel it until someone else joins. After that, it belongs to the table.
  • Selling tickets makes you a host. A host needs an active membership and a Stripe account. A host pays Stripe's fees and handles disputes on their own sales. After the first ticket sells, the only change a host can make is cancelling. A host who cancels refunds every buyer and pays OpenSeat a cancellation fee of 10% of what the event collected, at least $10.
  • Tribes are a host's audience. They are free to join. A Tribe host can charge dues through their own Stripe account; OpenSeat's fee on dues is 0%.

Conduct and safety

Treat people the way you would want to be treated at a table. Do not harass, threaten or harm anyone, and do not use OpenSeat for anything illegal.

  • Block anyone, any time. A block removes chat and visibility between the two of you.
  • Report a person or a message and OpenSeat's safety team reviews it. You will hear when your report is resolved; you will not be told what happened to the other person.
  • I need help is on every event, for anyone signed in.
  • No-shows: if someone at your table says they did not see you, it counts as a no-show. You can contest it through support. A warning comes at 2 no-shows. At 3 in a row, or 5 within 12 months, claiming seats is held until you verify your identity. There is no other penalty.
  • Serious complaints are acted on only once they are founded, never on a single unchecked report. Depending on what we find, we can limit what you can do on OpenSeat, up to closing your account. A referral to the authorities is always a decision made by a person.
  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number. OpenSeat is not an emergency service and cannot make that call for you.

The assistant

Every member has an assistant. She is called Eleanor unless you rename her. She is an AI, and she speaks as herself.

  • She does not speak for you. She does not write your messages, suggest replies or take part in your chats with other people.
  • She knows what you tell her, what a public event page shows, and what your friends have agreed to share with friends. She does not know who else is at an event or what happened there.
  • What she can do for you is set by the permissions you give her. With none set, she can talk with you but not act for you.
  • She can get things wrong. Check anything that matters before you rely on it.

What you post

Your photos, messages and profile stay yours. You give us permission to store them and show them to the people you chose, so OpenSeat can work. Post only what you have the right to share.

  • An event chat is deleted after 30 days with no new messages.
  • A photo with other people in it stays in the event chat, and anyone in that chat can remove it.

Our role

OpenSeat is where people find a table and each other. We do not run the venues, and we do not run hosts' events. Venues run their own places. Hosts run their own events and sell their own tickets. The people you meet are responsible for themselves, and you are responsible for you.

Limits

OpenSeat is in beta. We work hard to keep it running and safe, but it is provided as it is, and we cannot promise it will always be available or free of mistakes.

As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for what happens at a venue, at an event, or between members, and our responsibility to you is limited. Nothing here takes away rights the law gives you that cannot be taken away.

Ending your account

  • You can close your account at any time. See Delete your data for how, and for what happens to your information.
  • Closing an account ends membership at once, releases your seats and ends your unused credits. A Tribe you own is handed to a successor you name, or archived.
  • We can limit or close an account after a founded serious complaint, as described above.

Changes

We will update these terms as OpenSeat changes. Each version is numbered. When a change is material, we tell members and the app asks you to accept the new version before you carry on.
