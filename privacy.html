Who we are

OpenSeat is run by OpenSeat LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. Stephen Iervella, founder and CEO, is responsible for privacy at OpenSeat: accounts@openseat.ca.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, how you sign in (emailed link, Google, Facebook or passkey) and your first name. When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we take your first name and email from that account.
  • Sign-in records. When you sign in, we record the time, the method, whether it worked, your IP address and your browser's user agent. This helps us spot break-ins.
  • Your date of birth. Asked the first time you claim a seat, to keep OpenSeat adults only. Other members see an age decade at most, never your date of birth.
  • Your profile. Your username, a title, a description and a profile picture, if you add them, and your time zone.
  • What you share, and with whom. The choices you make at setup about who can see each thing, and your consent settings. Every change to a consent setting is recorded.
  • Seats and credits. The seats you claim and release, the events you attend, and your credit balance.
  • Messages and photos. What you write in event chats, Tribe chats and one-to-one chats, and photos you upload. We remove location and other hidden data from photos before they are shown.
  • Payments. Stripe handles every payment. We keep Stripe's reference numbers, your card's brand and last four digits, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of the card's Stripe fingerprint. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your device. A random device id, stored as a cookie. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy. Together with the card marker above, it stops someone from opening a new account to get around a block or a no-show hold.
  • Terms receipts. Each time you accept the Terms or this policy, we record which version, when, your IP address, your browser's user agent and how you accepted. This is our proof of what you agreed to and when.
  • The assistant. Your assistant's name, picture and settings. For each turn of a conversation, we record the AI model used and the provider's reference id, not the words. If you report one of her answers, we keep what you wrote in the report.
  • Notifications. If you allow push notifications, the token your device gives us to send them.
  • Safety. Reports you make or that involve you, and support conversations.

We do not collect your location. We do not ask your phone number. We do not ask your religion or sexual orientation.

Why we use it

  • To run OpenSeat: sign you in, show you events, hold your seats and credits, and run membership and tickets.
  • To keep it adults only, and to keep people safe: reports, blocks, the no-show rules and stopping duplicate accounts.
  • To let your assistant help you, within the permissions you give her.
  • To keep proof of what you agreed to, and to handle disputes and audits.

We do not sell your data. We do not run ads, and there are no analytics or tracking tools on openseat.ca or in the app.

Who sees what

  • Strangers do not see your name. Names show once you have shared a table.
  • Nobody gets a list of who is at an event.
  • A block hides you from each other from then on.
  • Your assistant knows what you tell her, what a public event page shows, and what your friends have agreed to share with friends. She does not know who else is at an event or what happened there.

Who helps us run OpenSeat

These companies process data for us, each for one job:

  • Linode hosts our servers. Our database, photos and backups live there.
  • Supabase runs sign-in. It holds your email address and your linked Google or Facebook sign-in, and sends sign-in emails.
  • Google and Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Anthropic runs the AI behind your assistant. Each message you send her goes to Anthropic with the recent conversation and her name. When you have given her permissions, the results of what she looks up for you go too, such as events she searched or facts from your own profile. Your name, email and account id are not sent.
  • Stripe processes payments for membership and credits. Ticket payments go to the host's own Stripe account.
  • Resend sends OpenSeat's emails other than sign-in emails.

We share data with others only when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

What you control

  • Permissions and privacy (You, then Settings): whether friends see the events you hold a seat at, whether your activity is open to people you approve, whether you show on a friend's booking screen, and whether followers need your approval.
  • Your assistant's permissions (You, then Settings, then Your assistant): she acts for you only within the permissions you switch on. With none on, she can talk with you but not act.
  • Blocks. Block anyone, any time.

How long we keep it

  • Most of your data is kept while your account is open.
  • Event chats are deleted after 30 days with no new messages.
  • Backups are made every night and kept for 14 days.
  • When you close your account, your data is held for 90 days in case you ask support to restore it, then deleted or made anonymous, as described under Delete your data.

Get a copy of your data

Email accounts@openseat.ca from the address on your account and ask for a copy. We will send you what we hold about you.

Delete your data

To delete your account and your data, email accounts@openseat.ca from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. This works whether you signed in with an email link, Google or Facebook.

When an account is closed:

  • You are signed out everywhere at once. Membership ends, your seats are released and unused credits end. A Tribe you own goes to a successor you name, or is archived.
  • For 90 days your data is held so support can restore the account if you ask. Released seats and ended credits do not come back.
  • After 90 days we delete your email address and sign-ins, sign-in records, profile, messages, photos, assistant settings and notifications, and remove your name from the account.
  • Some records are kept without your name or email: payment and credit records, seat history, safety reports and the messages attached to them, your date of birth with the age check, the scrambled device and card markers, and your terms receipts, including the IP address and user agent recorded with them. We keep these for accounting, safety and proof of consent.
  • Deleted data can remain in nightly backups for up to 14 days.

Cookies and storage

openseat.ca sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

The app at app.openseat.ca uses only what it needs to work:

  • Cookies that keep you signed in and protect your requests, and the device id described above, which lasts up to 400 days.
  • Your browser's own storage for your colour and theme choice, your share link, whether you declined a passkey, and, during sign-in only, the details needed to finish signing in.

No advertising or tracking cookies.

Adults only

OpenSeat is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 has an account, tell us at accounts@openseat.ca.

Changes

We will update this policy as OpenSeat changes. Each version is numbered. When a change is material, we tell members and the app asks you to accept the new version before you carry on.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data: accounts@openseat.ca. OpenSeat LLC. Privacy contact: Stephen Iervella.
